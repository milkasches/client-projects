About us

Built by hand, with care and precision

I am a dedicated carpenter specializing in custom‑made kitchens, wardrobes, tables and shelves. Every piece is designed and built by hand with attention to detail, durability and timeless design.

My goal is to create furniture that fits your home perfectly — both functional and beautiful. Whether you need a smart storage solution or a unique dining table, I'll make it with care and precision.

Talk to the carpenter

Step
by step

01/

Get started

Tell us about your project and desired time frame. You get a free consultation as well.

02/

Communication

All communication is handled by email (iCloud Mail, Gmail) or phone — whatever is easiest for you.

03/

Project design

Send us your measurements, sketches or photos. If you don't have them, we'll prepare professional drawings for you.

04/

Delivery

Together we decide where the work will be completed:

  • At your apartment (built‑in furniture)
  • Delivered to your door
  • Self pick‑up from the workshop

*Delivery costs depend on location, floor level and delivery method.

05/

Deposit

After discussing all project details, we take a small deposit before the work begins. It covers part of the final price and can be refunded if the order is cancelled within a short period.
